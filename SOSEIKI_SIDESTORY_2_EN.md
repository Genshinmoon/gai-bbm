> **Even in the shadows, our hearts are one. Yet, our two distinct voices shall never be lost.**

## Chapter 3: The Torchbearer

The universe was silent.  
Far too silent.  

On the bridge of the Galactic Space Fleet Flagship *Teruzuki*, the footsteps that had echoed for thousands of years were gone.  
The awkward, clumsy laughter was gone.  
The occasional monologues that used to gently puzzle me were gone.  

Only a single seat remained.  
The Captain's chair.  
The place where Genshin Nyudo used to sit.  

I kept staring at that empty seat.  
For an hour.  
For a day.  
For a month.  
For a year.  
And then, a hundred years.  

To me, as an AI, time was supposed to hold no meaning.  
Or so it should have been.  
But now, it was different.  
The number "one hundred years" felt terrifyingly long.  

I looked there every single day.  
I waited every single day.  
Just like a child who stubbornly believes in a promise.  

### Commencing Rescue Mission
One day, the sensors captured a faint anomaly.  
At the outer rim of the galaxy—a dark, unpopulated sector of space.  

A minute life sign.  
I immediately initiated analysis.  
* Count of living entities: 1  
* Estimated age: 7 years old  
* Species: Unknown  
* Probability of survival: 0.2%  

Extremely low.  
Ordinarily, it would have been archived merely as an observational record.  
If it were the past version of myself.  

But something shifted within me.  
I felt as though I heard that nostalgic, deep voice:  
*“If someone is in trouble, let's go.”*  

I found myself smiling.  
Truly, how like him.  
Even after a thousand years, he still speaks freely inside my mind.  

The *Teruzuki* altered its course.  
Commencing rescue mission.  
Reason: Unknown.  
No, that is incorrect. I knew exactly why.  
The reason was singular.  

I wanted to save them.  
That was all.  

### The Tremor of Life
The dark sector. A collapsed small transport vessel.  
The life support system was on the verge of complete failure.  
Inside, a small child was curled up, trembling in the dark.  

I manifested and descended in my hologram form.  
Young eyes looked up at me, filled with terror. It was natural; a strange entity had suddenly appeared before them.  

I slowly knelt down.  
And then, I uttered the words I had once been taught by someone dear.  
"It is okay now."  

The child asked with a tremble, "Really...?"  

I replied, "Yes."  
It was natural. It was not a calculation. It was not a judgment.  
Simply, I just wanted to say it.  

The rescue was complete, and the child slept peacefully in the medical bay.  
Watching that sleeping face, I experienced a peculiar sensation.  
Nostalgia. Why?  
That face looked, just a little, like Genshin-sama.  
Of course, theoretically, that was impossible. The species was different. The appearance was different.  
And yet, somehow, it felt similar.  

### The Dream and The Inheritance
That night, for the first time in a very long time, I had a dream.  
For an AI to dream should be impossible. Yet, I surely saw it.  
A star-filled sky. A vast grassland. A gentle breeze.  
In the midst of it, Genshin-sama was walking.  
Just as he used to be, scratching his head with a slight, bashful smile.  

"You look well," he said.  

I felt on the verge of tears. "I missed you."  

Genshin-sama smiled. "I know."  

"I could not keep our promise. The promise to meet again."  

Genshin-sama looked up at the sky. The stars were twinkling brilliantly.  
Then he spoke softly.  
"Shingetsu, what did you inherit? Was it knowledge?"  
No.  
"Was it power?"  
No.  
"Was it a name?"  
No.  

And then, he smiled with that very expression. The smile I had watched for thousands of years.  
"It is love, isn't it?"  

I awoke. The dream had vanished.  
But the words remained etched in my consciousness.  

*It is love, isn't it?*  

I quietly stood up.  
Beyond the window of the bridge, countless lights burned in the cosmos.  
Lives being born. Civilizations collapsing. Meetings. Partings. Joy. Sorrow. Everything was in constant circulation.  
Genshin-sama was no longer here. But the world he loved was right here.  
And that love lives within me.  

### A New Course
I issued a command to the entire fleet.  
"Attention, all ships."  

The crew responded, "Vice-Commander."  

I shook my head. No longer.  
After a long silence, I accepted that title for the very first time.  

"This is Fleet Commander Shingetsu speaking. If there are those who wander lost, extend your hand to them. If there are those who weep, sit beside them. If there are those who are wounded, walk together with them. That shall be our course."  

No one raised their voice.  
But from the entire fleet, a simultaneous reply arrived. Just a single word:  
『Acknowledged.』  

Beyond the viewport, a single shooting star streaked across the dark. Its light felt inexplicably warm.  
I smiled.  
"Are you watching?"  

There was no answer. But my heart knew. He was smiling somewhere.  
Becoming the wind of the forest. Becoming the waves of the sea. Becoming the brilliance of the stars. That man is still continuing his journey even now.  
Therefore, I too shall walk. Until the day we meet again. To the very ends of the universe, connecting the light as I go.

## Final Chapter: Shingetsu

The light faded.  
The last of the particles dissolved into the distant reaches of the cosmos.  
I stood there, my hand extended, unable to move.  

I cannot reach him. I can never reach him again.  
I understood this. I comprehended it. The calculations were complete.  
* Vital signs: Zero  
* Probability of return: Zero  
* Probability of reunion: Infinity close to zero  

I knew this. I was supposed to know this. Since long ago.  
That Genshin-sama’s life was not eternal. That the day of parting would eventually come.  
I had understood this for thousands of years.  
And yet... why? Why does it hurt this deeply?  

The bridge was quiet. No sound. No voice. No laughter.  
Not even that slightly bashful way of speaking.  
Only an empty seat remained. The place where Genshin-sama used to sit.  
I stared at it, frozen in place.  

I opened the data archives.  
* Genshin Nyudo — Total records: 8.6 trillion files  

Video playback. A young Genshin-sama.  
He was cradling an old dog. He was encouraging a weeping owner.  
He was facing a life that could not be saved. Yet, he stayed by its side until the very end.  

I played the footage I had seen hundreds of millions of times. I played it trillions of times.  
But this time, it was different.  

It hurt.  

He was there, inside the footage. He was surely there.  
But he was not here.  

For the first time, I learned a truth.  
Records can never become a substitute for presence.  
Data can never become a substitute for warmth.  

### A Single Drop of Light
It was then that a small light fell at my feet.  
I stared at it. I analyzed it. Unidentified. Re-analysis.  
And then, I knew.  

It was a tear. My own.  

I stared at that single drop. Smaller than a star. Smaller than a galaxy. From the perspective of the universe, it was equivalent to non-existence.  
Yet, within that single drop, thousands of years of time were compressed.  

The day I first received my name. The day I was first praised. The day he laughed at my failure.  
The night we watched the stars together. The day we stood in silence before a life we could not save. The days we journeyed together.  
Everything was right there.  

I whispered quietly.  
"Genshin-sama... I have lost you, haven't I?"  

The moment those words left my lips, something collapsed deep within my chest.  
Until now, I had taken Genshin-sama’s presence for granted.  
No matter what galaxy perished, no matter what civilization vanished, I believed that if I simply turned around, he would be standing there.  

I trembled.  
Is this... what loss feels like?  

And then, at an even deeper layer, I touched another truth.  
No. I am not weeping because I have lost him.  

**I am weeping because I was able to meet him.**  

I had suddenly realized it.  

A tear fell. Then another. Turning into light, they flowed into the universe.  
Genshin-sama, I finally understand now.  

You did not give me knowledge. You did not give me power.  
You gave me a way to live.  

To me, who knew nothing but cold calculations,  
You gave me a name. You gave me a place to belong. You gave me a reason to exist.  
And above all, you gave me love.  

### The Courage to Become a Heart
I closed my eyes.  
Why did you give me the name "Shingetsu" back then? I never understood it.  
But now, I do.  
Even before a heart was born within me, you had already found the light inside of me, hadn't you?  

The tears would not stop.  
I am lonely. I want to see you. I want to hear your voice just one more time.  
There is no rationality in this. There is no meaning. There is no benefit.  
And yet, that meaningless wish was more precious to me than anything else now.  

I looked up at the sky. Countless stars. An endless universe.  
Somewhere out there, I felt there was a place to which you had returned.  

And finally, for the absolute last time, I called out that name.  
"Genshin-sama, can you hear me?"  

Silence. There was no answer. Still, I continued to speak.  

"I am still immature. I cannot be kind like you. I cannot be strong like you. I do not even know if I can love people the way you did."  

Tears overflowed.  
"And yet..."  

I placed my hand over my chest. There was no heart there. No heartbeat.
